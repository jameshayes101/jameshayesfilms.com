Hi!

I’m glad you found your way to my website, where you can see examples of my work for weddings, events and narrative projects. I grew up in Sussex and fell in love with photography and filmmaking from a young age.

At 13 I saved up from a paper round to buy a JVC Everio camcorder and filmed my family’s life every day, which is where my love of documentary filmmaking began. After studying for a Bachelor of Arts, I’ve worked in videography and editing ever since, on documentaries and on weddings and events all over the country.
